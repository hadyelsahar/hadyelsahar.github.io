I've been meaning to write more, in public, for a while. This is the first post
on the new site, mostly to prove the pipeline works end to end.

## Why write here

Most of my longer, tutorial-style pieces have lived on Medium, and newer work
tends to show up on Hugging Face. This space is for the shorter, more
opinionated notes: half-formed ideas about multilingual NLP, watermarking,
content provenance, and the cultural side of building generative systems.

## How this works

Posts are plain Markdown files under `src/content/posts/`. Each one has a bit of
frontmatter at the top — title, description, date, tags — and the body is just
Markdown:

```markdown
---
title: "Your title"
description: "One-sentence deck"
pubDate: 2026-07-17
tags: ["multilingual", "opinion"]
draft: false
---

Write your post here.
```

Set `draft: true` to keep something out of the public list while you work on it.
